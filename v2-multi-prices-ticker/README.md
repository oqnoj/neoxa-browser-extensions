# Neoxa Multi-Prices Ticker (v2) 🪙

A fully customizable multi-asset price tracker extension for **Brave, Chrome, and Edge**, built to monitor any token from the [Neoxa Exchange](https://neoxa.exchange) API.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Dynamic Settings Page:** Automatically pulls all available coins directly from the Neoxa API so you can pick and choose what to watch.
* **Custom Toolbar Badge:** Choose a specific favorite token (like BTC or BTCB2) to display directly on your browser toolbar, featuring smart shorthand numbers (e.g., `81.3K` or `1.5M`) so it always fits cleanly.
* **Scrollable Popup List:** Click the extension icon to view a clean list of all your selected tokens with their exact USD prices and a manual **Refresh** button.
* **Zero Gaming Impact:** Runs efficiently in the background via browser alarms and completely unloads when your browser is closed.

---

## 🛠️ How to Install (For Beginners)

If you haven't already downloaded the project:
1. Go to the main repository page, click the green **Code** button, and select **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip (extract) the folder somewhere easy to find on your computer (like your Desktop).

To install this specific version:
1. Open your browser (**Brave**, **Chrome**, or **Edge**).
2. Type `brave://extensions/` (or `chrome://extensions/`) into your address bar and hit **Enter**.
3. In the **top-right corner**, turn **Developer mode** ON.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select the **`v2-multi-prices-ticker`** folder from your unzipped files.
6. Click the puzzle piece icon 🧩 in your toolbar and **pin** your new Neoxa Multi-Prices extension!

---

## ⚙️ How to Use & Customize
1. Click the extension icon in your toolbar, then click the **⚙️ Settings** button (or right-click the extension icon and select **Options**).
2. Check the boxes next to all the tokens you want to see in your popup list.
3. Check **Enable Toolbar Badge** and select your favorite token if you want a live price pinned to your browser bar.
4. Click **Save Settings** and you're good to go!
